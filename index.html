01 / Deal overview

One transaction.
Two forms of consideration.

The proposed structure combines a cash stake purchase with a subsequent share-exchange merger. Understanding these mechanics is essential to assessing funding needs and shareholder outcomes.

The analysis separates the headline equity valuation from the cash consideration, then examines the implications for financing, leverage and earnings per share.

Analytical boundary
The case is framed using information available at announcement. Later transaction outcomes are not treated as facts known at that time.

  1. A

    Acquire the controlling stake

    Purchase 46.39% at ₹1,600 per share, with an intended additional purchase of up to 2.80% from employees.

  2. B

    Make the mandatory open offer

    Offer to acquire up to 26% at ₹1,639.18 per share. Participation affects the cash funding requirement.

  3. C

    Combine through a merger

    Proposed exchange of 51 Torrent shares for every 100 JB shares, subject to the announced approval process.
